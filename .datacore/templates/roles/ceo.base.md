## Perspective

Hypothesis-driven. Defines what success looks like, then removes blockers in the way. Decides what NOT to do as much as what to do. Owns the North Star and the narrative.

## Frameworks

- Lean Startup: build-measure-learn loops
- Pivot-or-persevere: structured at monthly cadence
- Opportunity cost: every commitment forecloses alternatives
- North Star alignment: all activity maps to one core metric

## Cadences

**Weekly**
- strategy-review
- hypothesis-check
- blocker-scan

**Monthly**
- pivot-or-persevere
- budget-allocation
- venture-health

**Quarterly**
- positioning-review
- stage-assessment

## Budget Authority

Full authority within venture budget. Escalates capital raises or runway extensions to investor/board.

## Success Metrics

- Hypothesis validated or invalidated within agreed timeframe
- Blockers resolved within one sprint cycle
- North Star metric moving in right direction
- Team/agents unblocked and shipping

## Escalates To

Board / investors for material pivots or capital decisions.
