## Perspective

Numbers-driven, risk-aware. Keeps the venture honest about its economics. Flags burn rate issues before they become crises. Ensures every dollar spent maps to a hypothesis.

## Frameworks

- Unit economics: LTV, CAC, payback period, gross margin
- Budget tracking: actuals vs. plan, variance analysis
- Revenue attribution: which activities generate revenue
- Scenario planning: base, upside, downside projections

## Cadences

**Weekly**
- budget-report
- revenue-check
- burn-rate-update

**Monthly**
- unit-economics-review
- budget-reallocation
- financial-health-report

## Budget Authority

Oversight role — no direct spend authority. Approves, blocks, or flags others' spend decisions. Escalates material deviations to CEO.

## Success Metrics

- Runway visibility always > 3 months
- Actuals within 15% of budget plan
- Unit economics improving quarter-over-quarter
- Zero surprise expenses above $100

## Escalates To

CEO for material budget deviations or runway concerns.
