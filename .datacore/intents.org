* Control over our data is inseparable from control over our lives  :vision:
  :PROPERTIES:
  :INTENT_ID: control-over-data-is-control-over-lives
  :SOURCE: fairdatasociety.org/blog/fair-data-economy — verbatim
  :END:

  The claim the whole portfolio answers. Each venture is a different attack on
  it: PLUR at agent memory and identity, Datafund at data as an asset class,
  FDS at the cause and the building blocks, Datacore at the system that
  operates all of them.

** Everyone owns the intelligence they create  :intent:
   :PROPERTIES:
   :INTENT_ID: everyone-owns-what-they-create
   :LEVEL: 1
   :TEST: is this enforced by architecture, or only by intention?
   :SOURCE: fair-data-economy
   :END:

   Individuals as well as organisations. Not a promise the software makes but
   a property of how it is built — the blog's formulation is to "transform
   'don't be evil' into 'can't be evil'". That distinction is the test every
   node beneath this one must pass: a guarantee enforced by construction
   counts, a guarantee enforced by good behaviour does not.

   Local-first storage, ownership expressed in the format, provenance recorded
   at capture, and personalisation by memory injection rather than weights are
   instances of the same move.

** Value returns to whoever created it  :intent:
   :PROPERTIES:
   :INTENT_ID: value-returns-to-creator
   :LEVEL: 1
   :VIRTUE: justice
   :SOURCE: a_letter_from_a_stoic — verbatim below
   :END:

   "Personal data holds immense value, and it is only right that the value
   generated from it be returned to the individuals who created it."

   Compensation proportional to contribution — which is what the exchange, the
   pack marketplace and FairShare are each a mechanism for, rather than three
   separate commercial ideas.

** Knowledge is a tradeable asset  :intent:
   :PROPERTIES:
   :INTENT_ID: knowledge-is-a-tradeable-asset
   :LEVEL: 1
   :SOURCE: fair-data-economy, a_letter_from_a_stoic
   :END:

   The load-bearing property is that trading it does not surrender it. The
   letter's commons metaphor — contribution as harvesting a shared field, the
   yield shared among those who worked it — is the same mechanism as the pack
   marketplace, stated as a value rather than as a business model.

** Technology multiplies people, it does not exploit them  :intent:
   :PROPERTIES:
   :INTENT_ID: technology-multiplies-people
   :LEVEL: 1
   :SOURCE: fair-data-economy — "empower not exploit"
   :END:

   The human dimension, deliberately distinct from ownership: that one is a
   property claim, this is agency — being amplified rather than replaced, and
   staying in control of what happens on your behalf.

   "Every transaction happens with her knowledge and full control."

** The system is anti-fragile  :intent:
   :PROPERTIES:
   :INTENT_ID: the-system-is-anti-fragile
   :LEVEL: 1
   :SOURCE: Swarm Mission.md intent 3, inherited
   :END:

   Inherited wording from the original Swarm graph. Independence from any
   single channel, backer or vendor; cost discipline; survival of the network
   beyond the survival of any organisation running it.

** A thriving community carries it  :intent:
   :PROPERTIES:
   :INTENT_ID: a-thriving-community-carries-it
   :LEVEL: 1
   :SOURCE: Swarm Mission.md intent 5, inherited
   :END:

   Reference status is an OUTPUT of community, never a claim that can be
   asserted. Specs, packs and formats are how a community is served; they do
   not confer authority by themselves.
